# Roadmap

Delivered: **Phase 1 + Payroll** and **Phase 2** (service desk, travel & expenses, compensation & benefits, e-signature). See the README. Each later module plugs into the existing employee master, workflow engine,
notifications, documents, RBAC and audit, so none needs new infrastructure.

## Phase 2: Service delivery & rewards (delivered)

- **HR service desk:**
  - service catalogue, tickets with reference numbers, routing, priority/SLA and escalation;
  - internal notes, knowledge base, reopen and CSAT;
  - service KPIs (resolution time, SLA %, backlog, satisfaction).
- **Compensation:** salary review and increment planning, bonus schemes, total rewards statements.
- **Benefits:** eligibility, enrolment, health insurance, cost analysis.
- **Travel & expenses:**
  - travel requests, per diem, advances and their retirement;
  - expense claims with receipts;
  - departmental budgets.
- **E-signature integration** for contracts and letters.

## Phase 3: Talent & lifecycle

- **Recruitment / ATS:** requisitions with approval, career portal, internal vacancies, screening, interviews and scorecards, references, offers, talent pool, analytics.
- **Onboarding:**
  - automatic checklists for HR, IT, finance and admin;
  - policy acknowledgment;
  - probation milestones and reviews;
  - new-hire surveys.
- **Performance:**
  - cycles, role-specific templates, cascading objectives and KPIs, competencies;
  - self, manager and 360-degree reviews;
  - PIPs, calibration, acknowledgments.
- **Learning (LMS):** course catalogue, requests and budgets, attendance, e-learning and quizzes, CPD tracking, training needs analysis, effectiveness.
- **Employee relations:** grievances and disciplinary cases with restricted visibility, investigations, hearings, appeals, outcomes.
- **Assets:** issue, acknowledgment, return and recovery on exit.
- **Offboarding:** multi-department clearance, exit interviews, final settlement integration.
- **Engagement:** pulse surveys with anonymity thresholds, suggestion box, recognition.
- **OHS:** incidents and near misses, investigations and corrective actions, with an occupational-health data zone separated from HR.

## Phase 4: Enterprise intelligence & SaaS

- **Planning:** succession planning (critical roles, readiness, single-person dependencies), workforce planning and scenarios.
- **Analytics:** custom report builder, predictive turnover and retirement analytics.
- **Identity and integrations:** AD/LDAP and SSO (Microsoft 365), finance/ERP connectors, BI warehouse feed, LHIMS integration (minimum data).
- **Governed AI assistant:** policy Q&A over approved documents, natural-language reporting and drafting, with permission-aware retrieval and human approval.
- **Multi-tenant SaaS:** tenant onboarding and billing, subdomain routing, PostgreSQL RLS, per-tenant branding.
