# User guide (quick reference by role)

## Employees

- **Install on your phone:** open the site in Chrome, then use the menu → *Add to Home screen*.
- **Leave:** My leave → *Apply for leave*. The form shows working days, your balance and your return date before you submit. Track progress under *My requests* ("waiting on …").
- **Clock in/out:** My attendance → *Clock in*. If you forgot, use *Correct* on that day.
- **Payslips:** My payslips. The PDF password (if enabled) is your staff number or date of birth (DDMMYYYY).
- **Letters:** My documents & letters → *Request a letter* (e.g. confirmation of employment for a bank).
- **Wrong details?** My profile → *Request a correction*. HR verifies before it changes. Emergency contacts can be updated directly.
- **Your data (Act 843):** My profile → *Download my data*.

- **Help desk:** HR help desk → *New request*. Pick the service, and you'll see related knowledge-base articles. Track replies and rate the service when it's resolved.
- **Travel:** Travel & expenses → *Request travel* (per diem is calculated for you). After the trip, submit an *expense claim* linked to the trip within 14 days; any unspent advance is recovered from your salary.
- **Benefits:** Benefits & total rewards → enrol in eligible plans, add dependants, download your total rewards statement.
- **Signatures:** Documents to sign → read the document, type your full name and confirm.
- **Internal jobs:** Internal job openings → *Apply*. Your details come from your employee record.
- **New starters:** Onboarding tasks shows your checklist (policies to acknowledge, details to confirm, training, a short feedback survey) and what HR, IT and your supervisor are preparing for you.
- **Appraisal:** My performance → open the current cycle. Add objectives (weights total 100%, link them to a goal) and submit them to your supervisor; at year end rate yourself with evidence and submit. When your review is released, read it and acknowledge; if you disagree, say why and HR follows up.
- **Learning:** My learning shows your training (mandatory items first), the course catalogue (request or enrol; paid courses need approval), your CPD credits against your licence requirement, and your development plan. E-learning: work through the modules, then pass the assessment (3 attempts). Rate each course when you finish.
- **360 feedback:** Feedback requests lists colleagues who asked for your view. Answers are confidential and only shown combined with at least two others.
- **Interview panels:** if you sit on a panel, Interview panels lists your interviews; open the CV and submit a scorecard afterwards.

## Line managers / department heads

- **Approvals inbox:** approve, return for correction (with a comment) or reject. Leave requests show the balance and any staffing clash.
- **My team:** reports, who's on leave, probation and contract dates. **Team attendance today** shows who is in, late or absent.
- **Delegations:** before you travel, delegate your approvals to a colleague for the dates you are away.
- **Roster** (if you manage shifts): assign shifts, generate from a pattern, then **Publish**.
- **Staff requisitions:** request new or replacement staff with the business need and budget. Approval goes to the head of department, HR Director and finance.
- **Probation reviews:** you are reminded a week before each review. Rate the six criteria and recommend confirmation, extension (with the gaps) or termination; HR decides through the inbox.
- **Team appraisals:** agree (or set) objectives, record the mid-year check-in, then complete the manager review. Ratings of *Needs improvement* or lower need comments and a development plan; open an **Improvement plan** with measurable milestones and support, which HR approves before the employee sees it.
- **Team learning:** nominate staff for courses, set up mentoring, turn appraisal gaps into development goals, and rate how completed training is being applied (you are reminded 30 days after completion).
- **Onboarding tasks:** day-one welcome, departmental induction and probation objectives for your new starters.

## HR officers

- **New employee:** Employees → *New employee* (choose an approved position). Then add bank details, upload documents and *Create user account*.
- **Personnel actions:** promotion, transfer, salary change and so on via the employee record → *Personnel action*. Use future dates for planned changes.
- **Exits:** *Record separation*. On the exit date the account is disabled, and retention dates are set.
- **Letters:** employee record → *Generate letter*, or approve self-service letter requests in the inbox.
- **Imports, data quality, leave adjustments, holidays and policies** are under People, Leave and Settings.

## Recruitment officers

- **Vacancies:** advertise an approved requisition (*Advertise*), add the description and screening questions, then *Publish*. *Career link* copies the public page address.
- **Pipeline:** sort by screening score; the flag shows knock-out answers and missing requirements, which are advisory. Select candidates and *Move* them; rejections need a reason and can send the regret email.
- **Candidate page:** schedule interviews (the panel and candidate are notified), record reference and background checks (tick consent first), then *Make offer*. After approval, *Send* emails a secure link; when the candidate accepts, *Hire* creates the employee and starts onboarding.
- **Talent pool:** Candidates & talent pool lists people who agreed to be kept on file. Data is anonymised automatically after the retention period (12 months by default).

## Training officer

Under **Training administration**: maintain the catalogue (mark courses mandatory for roles with a refresher period, link BLS-type courses to the clinical competency they renew), add e-learning modules and questions, schedule sessions and record attendance and scores, assign courses to people or departments, set budgets, and verify CPD logged by staff. **Compliance & effectiveness** shows the mandatory-training matrix, skills gaps from appraisals and spend, ratings and on-the-job impact.

## HR: performance cycles

Create a cycle (period, deadlines, departments, target distribution) and **Launch** it: each eligible employee gets an appraisal on the matching form with their supervisor as reviewer. Move it on with the phase button (mid-year → year-end review → calibration → release). In calibration, open appraisals to confirm or adjust ratings (a reason is required and audited) and compare the distribution with the target. Forms, competencies and rating scales are under *Forms, competencies & scales*. Salary review guidelines can include a `rating` (e.g. rating 5 → 8–12%) so merit proposals follow the latest final rating.

## HR service desk agents

Work the **Ticket queue** by due time. *Ask employee for information* pauses the SLA clock. Use internal notes for anything the employee must not see, *Generate letter* when the service has a template, and *Resolve* with a clear resolution. Log phone and walk-in requests on the employee's behalf.

## Finance (travel & expenses)

After the inbox approvals, open **Travel & claims admin**. Use *Pay advance* to record the transfer and *Settle* to close a claim, either with the next salary (reimbursement or recovery) or by transfer with a reference. You cannot settle a claim you approved.

## Payroll officer (preparer)

1. Attendance → *Period summaries* → **Build & lock** for the month.
2. Payroll → Runs → **New period**, then **New run** (regular).
3. Add one-off adjustments (bonus, arrears) if needed. Use *Compute retro arrears* for back-dated increments.
4. **Calculate.** Fix every **error**, review **warnings** and the **variance** report.
5. **Submit for approval.**

## Payroll approver (checker, must be a different person)

1. Open the run from the inbox. Review the totals, the variance and the GRA/SSNIT reports.
2. **Approve** (or return with comments).
3. Download the **bank file** and MoMo file, then upload them to the bank.
4. **Release & publish payslips.** This locks the run and posts loan repayments.
5. File the PAYE schedule and SSNIT report, and post the GL journal.

## Auditors

Audit trail (with *Verify integrity*), sensitive access review, and read-only access to payroll and HR reports.
